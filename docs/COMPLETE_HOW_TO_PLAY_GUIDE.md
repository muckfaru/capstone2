## 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Launch CyberArena
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email, password (6+ characters), and a username
4. Choose an avatar from the gallery
5. Verify your email if prompted
6. You're in!

**Google Sign-In:** Click the Google button to sign in with your Google account instead.

### Logging In

1. Click **"Login"**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click the login button

### First-Time Experience

New players automatically enter the **3D Story Mode** — an immersive narrative that introduces you to cybersecurity concepts. After the story, you'll arrive at the Landing Page.

## 3. 3D Story Mode

### Overview

A narrative introduction where you explore a 3D apartment, download a suspicious game, watch your computer get infected with malware, and get recruited into a secret cybersecurity organization.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W/A/S/D** | Move forward/left/backward/right |
| **Mouse** | Look around |
| **Shift** | Run (faster movement) |
| **E** | Interact with objects |
| **Space / Enter** | Advance dialogue |

### Story Flow

1. **Visual Novel Intro** — Comic-style panels tell the story: you want a new game but can't afford it, so you search for a "free download"
2. **Apartment Exploration** — Walk around your room in first person. Approach the computer desk
3. **Computer Desktop** — A simulated desktop appears. Open the browser and download the "free game"
4. **Malware Infection** — Popup windows flood the screen! Your computer appears to crash
5. **Recruitment** — A hologram character "Anonymouse" appears, explaining you've been hacked
6. **Decision** — Accept the invitation to join CyberArena (cybersecurity training) or decline

After the story, you arrive at the Landing Page to begin your training.

## 4. Beginner Tutorials

### Tutorial 1: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

**What You Learn:** The CIA Triad and threat modeling  
**Duration:** ~10 minutes  
**Interface:** CMD-style terminal with green text

**How to Play:**
1. Read through sections about Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability
2. Learn about Threats vs Vulnerabilities vs Risk
3. Answer 4 quiz questions
4. Each correct answer = 50 points

**Key Concepts:**
- **Confidentiality:** Only authorized users see data (encryption, passwords)
- **Integrity:** Data stays accurate and unmodified (digital signatures, checksums)
- **Availability:** Systems work when needed (backups, DDoS protection)
- **Threat** = potential danger; **Vulnerability** = weakness; **Risk** = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact

**XP:** Up to 100 points → 200 XP

---

### Tutorial 2: Network Basics

**What You Learn:** IP addresses, ports, and protocols  
**Duration:** ~10 minutes  
**Interface:** CMD terminal with command input

**How to Play:**
1. Learn about private vs public IP addresses
2. Understand port numbers (normal vs suspicious)
3. Compare protocols (HTTP vs HTTPS, TCP vs UDP)
4. Answer 3 quiz questions

**Key Concepts:**
- **Private IP (192.168.x.x):** Local network only
- **Public IP:** Visible on the internet
- **Port 80/443:** Normal (HTTP/HTTPS); **Port 4444:** Suspicious backdoor
- **HTTPS** is encrypted and safe; **HTTP** is not

**XP:** Up to 150 points → 200 XP

---

### Tutorial 3: Drop Zone Defender

**What You Learn:** Classifying network data vs attacks  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes per attempt  
**Interface:** Drag-and-drop game

**How to Play:**
1. Threats appear on screen across 8 waves
2. Drag each threat to the correct zone: **Data Layer** or **Network Layer**
3. Beat the 180-second timer
4. You have 5 hearts (shields) — wrong answers cost hearts

**Assets to Defend:** Employee PC, Database, Router, Email Server, Backup, CEO Laptop  
**Threats:** Phishing, Brute Force, Malware, DDoS, SQL Injection, Ransomware, Zero-Day, Insider Threat

**Scoring:** Correct classifications × points per wave  
**Max Score:** 500  
**XP:** 100–200 based on performance

**What You Learn:** Identify 6 types of cybersecurity threats  
**Duration:** 90 seconds (timed!)  
**Interface:** Drag-and-drop incident reports

**How to Play:**
1. Read 6 incident report descriptions
2. Drag each report to the correct threat category
3. Beat the 90-second timer!

**Threat Categories:**

- **Malware Attack** — Malicious software

**Score Formula:** `Completion Score + Time Bonus = Final Score (max 100)`

## 5. Intermediate Tutorials

### Tutorial 6: Encryption Basics (Caesar Cipher)

**What You Learn:** How encryption works, from Caesar Cipher to ransomware  
**Duration:** ~12 minutes  
**Interface:** Interactive Caesar Cipher wheel

**How to Play:**
1. **Learn Phase:** Watch the cipher wheel demonstration
2. **Practice Phase:** Drag the cipher wheel to set shift values, decrypt 3 messages
3. **Challenge Phase:** Encrypt 3 messages by finding the correct shift
4. **Ransomware Lesson:** Learn why AES-256 is uncrackable vs the 26-key Caesar Cipher

**Example:** Key = 3 → A becomes D, "HELLO" becomes "KHOOR"

**XP:** 100–200  
**GameMode Note:** Time-only leaderboard (no score column)

---

### Tutorial 7: Crypt Contract

**What You Learn:** Encryption key generation and secure messaging  
**Duration:** 5 missions × 3 messages each  
**Interface:** Phone encryption simulator

**How to Play:**
1. Create a custom encryption key (RSA-style concept)
2. Receive an encrypted message from your "boss"
3. Decrypt the message using your key
4. Read the decoded message
5. Encrypt your reply and send it
6. Avoid "police detection" — random checks with time pressure

**Lives:** 4 hearts (lose on police detection × 4)  
**Max Score:** 500  
**XP:** 100–200

---

### Tutorial 8: Phishing Detection Lab

**What You Learn:** Spot fake emails and avoid scams  
**Duration:** 90 seconds (timed!)  
**Interface:** Gmail-style email inbox

**How to Play:**
1. Read 8 emails carefully
2. For each email, choose: **Reply** (safe), **Spam** (phishing), or **Delete**
3. Beat the 90-second timer

**Red Flags (Phishing):**
- Generic greetings ("Dear Customer")
- Urgent/threatening language ("Account suspended!")
- Fake domains (paypa1.tk instead of paypal.com)
- Suspicious links (bit.ly, weird URLs)
- Requests for passwords/credit cards

**Legitimate Signs:**
- Uses your real name
- Official company domain (@netflix.com)
- Specific details (order numbers, dates)

**Scoring:** +150 points per correct, -10 per error  
**Max Score:** 1,200 (8 × 150)  
**XP:** 100–200

---

### Tutorial 9: Incident Commander (Cipher Defense Terminal)

**What You Learn:** Use encryption defense commands in a SOC terminal  
**Duration:** 10 waves  
**Interface:** Terminal command line

**How to Play:**
1. Threats spawn with crypto weaknesses (e.g., "weak_key", "ecb_pattern")
2. Type the correct command to fix the vulnerability (e.g., `enforce-aes256`, `switch-to-cbc`)
3. Correct command destroys the threat; wrong command damages your systems (-8 HP)
4. Manage 3 systems' health across 10 waves

**Sample Commands:**
- `enforce-aes256` — Fix weak key
- `switch-to-cbc` — Fix ECB mode
- `rotate-keys` — Fix key reuse
- `enable-hmac` — Fix missing authentication

**Power-ups (auto-drop):** Health (50%), Time Stop (30%), Destroy All (20%)  
**Max Score:** 500  
**XP:** 100–200

## 7. Additional Learning Experiences

### Asymmetric Cryptography Tutorial

**What You Learn:** How RSA public/private keys solve the key distribution problem  
**Phases:** INTRO → SYMMETRIC_PROBLEM → ASYMMETRIC_CONCEPT → KEY_GENERATION → PUBLIC_KEY_SHARE → ENCRYPTION_DEMO → DECRYPTION_DEMO → COMPLETE_EXCHANGE → REAL_WORLD → QUIZ  
**Features:** Visual Alice/Bob/Eve demonstration, one-way functions explained

### CipherShield (Symmetric Encryption Defense)

**What You Learn:** Why encryption key strength matters  
**Phases:** WAITING → ENCRYPTING → TRANSMITTING → INTERCEPTED → DECRYPTING → SUCCESS/FAILED  
**Mechanics:** Encrypt messages, transmit over network, attacker tries to break weak keys. 4 levels with increasing interception chance.

### Hash Integrity Game

**What You Learn:** Hash functions for tamper detection  
**Mechanics:** File transfer simulation — select hash algorithm (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256), check for tampering. 10 levels. Learn that MD5 is broken, SHA-256 is secure.

### DES vs AES Battle

**What You Learn:** Why DES is obsolete and AES is modern standard  
**Phases:** TUTORIAL → DES_PHASE (fails despite effort) → AES_PHASE (succeeds) → VICTORY  
**Mechanic:** Type encryption commands to strengthen encryption under enemy attack.

### Data Vault (Firewall Training)

**What You Learn:** Allow/deny network traffic decisions  
**Phases:** TUTORIAL (8 obvious scenarios) → 4 levels of increasingly complex mixed traffic  
**Mechanics:** Inspect IPs, protocols, behaviors. Identify APTs, insider threats, reconnaissance among legitimate traffic.

### Digital Forensics Scene

**What You Learn:** Terminal-based forensics investigation  
**Mechanics:** Use Windows commands (tasklist, wmic, etc.) to investigate incidents. Collect evidence, evaluate attack types, choose response actions.

### Incident Response Terminal Trainer

**What You Learn:** CMD commands for malware removal  
**Phases:** tasklist → tasklist /v → wmic query → taskkill → file removal → registry cleanup → recovery  
**Commands Used:** `tasklist`, `netstat -ano`, `taskkill /F /IM`, `reg delete`, `del /F /Q`, `schtasks /delete`

### Malware Defense

**What You Learn:** Identify malware types and select defenses  
**Phases:** Detection → Identification → Containment → Removal → Prevention  
**Tools:** Antivirus Scan, Network Isolation, Process Manager, Firewall Rules, Malware Removal, Backup Restore, Patch Manager

### Trojan Horse Walkthrough

**What You Learn:** How trojans are created and deployed (hacker's perspective)  
**Phases:** CREATE (payload) → DISGUISE (fake installer) → DEPLOY (distribution) → EXECUTE (victim runs) → BACKDOOR (access) → COMPLETE  
**Defense Tips:** Shown at each phase (antivirus scanning, certificate verification, avoid torrents)

## 8. Competitive Multiplayer Games

### Defuse the Trojan (2–3 Players)

**Concept:** Type cybersecurity keywords to destroy approaching enemies  

**How to Play:**
1. **Join a room** — Create or join via the Defuse the Trojan lobby
2. **Ready up** — Click Ready; host starts when everyone is ready
3. **Loading screen** — Synchronized countdown before match
4. **Type to fight!** — Enemies approach from the top of the screen, each labeled with a keyword
5. **Type the keyword** — Each keystroke fires a visible projectile
6. **Complete the word** — Destroys the enemy and scores points
7. **Wave clear** — All enemies must be destroyed before the next wave begins

**Typing Mechanics:**
- Type any enemy's keyword to target it
- Switch targets by typing a different enemy's word
- Progress is stored per enemy — return and continue typing
- Priority: closest enemy to your player (bottom of screen)

**Enemy Types:** Virus, Worm, Trojan, Ransomware  
**Keywords:** 40 IT-323 curriculum terms (firewall, antivirus, encryption, phishing, mfa, hash, salt, bruteforce, etc.)  

**UI:** Health bar, score, wave counter, combo counter, typed text display, target word hint  
**Multiplayer:** Host validates kills, all peers see each other's projectiles  
**Postgame:** Cards showing per-player WPM, accuracy, and longest streak

---

### Code Breaker (1v1)

**Concept:** Type code snippets accurately to damage your opponent  

**How to Play:**
1. **Join a room** — Create or join via the Code Breaker lobby
2. **Ready up** — Client clicks Ready, host starts match
3. **Loading screen** — Countdown before match
4. **Type code** — A code snippet appears; type it exactly (case-sensitive, spaces matter)
5. **Correct character:** +3 score, -2 opponent HP, green flash
6. **Wrong character:** -3 score, restart current line, red flash
7. **Win when** opponent's HP reaches 0, you finish the code first, or time expires with higher score

**Game Details:**
- **Starting Health:** 100 HP each
- **Match Time:** 3–4 minutes
- **Snippet Timer:** 15 seconds per code block
- **Power-ups:** Heal (+10 HP), Freeze Time (15s), Extend Time (+15s), Shield (15s invincibility)
- **Session persistence:** If you disconnect, you can resume the match later

---

### Akashic TCG (1v1)

**Concept:** Security-themed trading card game  

**How to Play:**
1. **Join a room** — Create or join via the Akashic TCG lobby
2. **Build your deck** from security concept cards (Firewall, Encryption, MFA, etc.)
3. **Play turn-based strategy** — Use resources to play cards
4. **Attack** opponent's System Integrity or Firewall bars
5. **30-second turn timer** — Auto-pass if no action taken

**Features:**
- Card rarity and special effects
- Resource management per turn
- Cosmetic card backs and break animations
- Session save/resume support

## 9. Teacher / Classroom Mode

### Creating a Game Mode Room (Teacher)

1. Open the **Teacher Dashboard** from the landing page
2. Select **"Game Mode"**
3. Choose a minigame from the catalog (any of the 13+ games)
4. Set player count (up to 50)
5. *(Optional)* Check **"Restrict by Student Number"** and enter allowed student numbers
6. Click **Create** — a room code is generated
7. Share the room code with students

### Creating a Multiple Choice Quiz (Teacher)

1. Select **"Multiple Choice"**
2. Create questions with 4 answer choices each
3. Set time per question and total question count
4. *(Optional)* Restrict by student number
5. Click **Create** — students join with room code

### Joining a Room (Student)

1. Click **"Join Room"** on the landing page
2. Enter the room code shared by your teacher
3. If the room has student number restriction, enter your student number
4. Wait in the lobby until the teacher starts

### During a Game Mode Session

- Teacher sees a live player list with avatars and ranks
- Teacher clicks **Start** → all students launch the game simultaneously
- Students play the minigame and submit scores automatically
- After completion, a **leaderboard** shows all student results
- Teacher can view the leaderboard with rankings, scores, and times

### Adding Students After Creation

1. In the lobby, click the **"+ Student"** button
2. Enter comma-separated student numbers in the popup
3. Click **Add** — newly added students can now join the room

## 13. Tips & FAQ

### General Tips

1. **Start with tutorials** — They're fun and the best way to level up
2. **Read carefully** — Many games are timed, but accuracy matters more than speed
3. **Use hints** — Most tutorials provide hints with no penalty
4. **Practice typing** — Defuse the Trojan and Code Breaker reward typing speed + accuracy
5. **Complete all tutorials** — Each one unlocks the next and teaches something new

### Multiplayer Tips

- **Defuse the Trojan:** Focus on closer enemies first (they're more dangerous). Switch targets if another enemy is closer
- **Code Breaker:** Read the code snippet before typing. Accuracy beats speed — wrong keys restart the line!
- **Akashic TCG:** Manage your resources carefully and save powerful cards for the right moment

### FAQ

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**  
A: Yes, for authentication, multiplayer, and score tracking.

**Q: Can I replay tutorials?**  
A: Yes! You can replay any unlocked tutorial to improve your score.

**Q: What happens if I disconnect during multiplayer?**  
A: Code Breaker and Akashic TCG save sessions — you can resume. Defuse the Trojan matches end if you leave.

**Q: How do I join my teacher's game?**  
A: Click "Join Room" on the landing page, enter the room code from your teacher, and wait for the game to start.

**Q: Can teachers see my score?**  
A: Yes, teachers see a live leaderboard with all student scores and times.

**Q: How do I earn CyberCoins?**  
A: Complete tutorials, earn XP, and claim the daily login bonus.

**Q: What if I forgot my password?**  
A: Use the password reset option on the login screen.
